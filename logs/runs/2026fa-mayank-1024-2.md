## Executive summary

A run-log entry for the first live run of a new mode that places job-posting pay ranges on the four government wage levels that now weight the H-1B lottery. For a fictional new-grad backend engineer in Boston it read 21 company job boards and placed 11 of 13 matching postings: 6 have a range crossing a level line, and 5 start below the lowest level. It refused the rest, saying why. One location-mapping bug was found on live data and fixed before the final run. The person-owned checks are still open.
