## Executive summary

A first sample run of a new checklist for choosing which job postings are worth an application, for an international Information Systems master's student targeting Business Intelligence Analyst roles. It was run on one real Boston company and one real posting. It found real sponsorship history and a live posting, and then three traps: the posting's title hides that it is a *Senior* role needing 5+ years; the company's sponsorship record is for a Business Intelligence *Engineer*; and the repository's own liveness checker called a made-up job on that company's board "active". It stopped without a score on purpose. Nothing here is personal data.
