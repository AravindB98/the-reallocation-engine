## Executive summary

This run checked 9 real US job postings for a design-engineer on a student visa, drawn from 1,373 jobs on 13 company job boards, against the visa-sponsorship file and the government occupation table. It dropped 2 postings as not interface work, left 1 for a person to open, and gave 2 Apply, 1 Consider and 3 Skip. Two of the skips were "New Grad" design roles that accept only 2027 graduates. It refused an AI-resilience score for 4 postings because the government row is blank. The author still has to re-read every posting, confirm the AI-drafted fields and fit values, and record a decision on each Apply and Consider row.
