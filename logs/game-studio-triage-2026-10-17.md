# Game-studio triage — 2026-10-17

## Executive summary

This is a shortlist of companies that may be video-game employers and have a history of sponsoring foreign workers, built for a student who has to decide where to spend a limited number of job applications.

Read it if you are choosing which game companies to apply to and you need to know which ones have actually sponsored someone before, rather than which ones say they are open to it.

What it found: the company list it searched describes 30,369 companies using 24 industry labels, and **not one of those labels is about games** — so games companies cannot be looked up directly and have to be guessed at from names and job titles. Searching that way turned up 266 possible game companies. Of those, only **13** have any record of sponsoring a worker, and only **4** have ever sponsored someone in the kind of job this student does. After filtering, **13** are worth a closer look. Every one of them still needs a person to open the company's website and confirm it makes games — the search cannot tell the difference between a game studio and a fitness company with "Interactive" in its name.

## What a person still has to do

1. **Confirm each company makes games.** Open the website. The search cannot tell a game studio from any company with "Studio", "Interactive" or "Unity" in its name or job titles.
2. **Check the posting is live.** `npm run ats:liveness -- <job-url>`. This is a gate: a dead posting ends the row.
3. **Check the dates against your own timeline.** This report contains no immigration advice and no immigration dates.
4. **Treat a blank sponsorship record as unknown, not as a no.** Most companies in the source file have no sponsorship record at all.
