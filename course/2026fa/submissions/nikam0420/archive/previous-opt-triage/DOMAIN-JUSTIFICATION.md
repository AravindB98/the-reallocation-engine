# Domain justification

## Executive summary
This recipe is designed for an international MSIS student approaching graduation and targeting US Data Analyst roles. It reveals stored sponsorship evidence alongside assumed hiring timing so the student can decide where to spend limited research effort. It demonstrates conditional triage, not a promise of sponsorship or a legal authorization assessment.

The information asymmetry is specific: a job title and company brand do not reveal whether a stored dataset contains approvals, whether those approvals relate to the target role, or whether an assumed hiring process can fit a deadline. The sponsorship layer supplies exact stored company fields. The scorer's liveness and timeline multipliers show why a promising company can still be a poor application target. A fictional 44-day window and 21-day hiring lag make the comparison concrete without publishing the author's real immigration details.

Within the 3-3-2 day, this automates a small part of the two research-and-apply hours: exact company lookup, explicit assumption checks and an auditable summary. An illustrative estimate of 5 minutes saved for 10 roles is 50 minutes per week; it is an estimate, not measured productivity. Closed postings may become networking research targets during the three networking hours, but the program does not identify contacts or send messages. A working adapter, tests and honest audit also support the three credibility-building hours.

Two domain-specific errors matter. First, aggregate approvals and a list including Data Analyst can be misread as approval of the current vacancy. A new graduate unfamiliar with employer/legal entity differences would find this especially hard to detect; the report limits the claim to the stored row and uses a conditional sponsorship tier. Second, an assumed 21-day hiring lag can omit offer processing, start-date negotiation or authorization constraints. A student new to US hiring may mistake arithmetic feasibility for legal eligibility; the prototype explicitly leaves actual timing for human/advisor review.

This small sample does not measure current hiring speed, verify live vacancies, join specific SOC-level filings, or establish legal OPT dates. Funding and wages are outside its decision. Those boundaries are part of the result, not hidden deficiencies.
