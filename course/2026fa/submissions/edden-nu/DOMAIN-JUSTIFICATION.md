# Domain justification

## Executive summary

This recipe helps early-career Information Systems applicants separate company-level sponsorship history from evidence about individual software, analytics, and design openings. It converts a small set of inputs into a transparent research queue, preserving missing evidence rather than inventing eligibility.

## Situation and information gap

Applicants exploring several technical role families face an unusually easy mistake: interpreting an employer's software-engineering sponsorship history as support for an analyst or designer vacancy. Company aggregates rarely reveal the current opening's policy, seniority, or timing. Missing records can also be mistaken for refusals. These gaps matter before applicants spend their limited time tailoring materials.

The recipe connects **80 Days to Stay** company data to **Job-Ops** page classification. Its sample uses the repository's fictional Aarav Patel persona and invented openings, not a public copy of a real student's résumé. The target workflow is personally relevant to the student's career interests; demonstration details are fictional and are not the student's immigration circumstances.

## Place in a 3-3-2 day

Within the two research-and-apply hours, the prototype organizes company lookup, posting-state checks, timing assumptions, and next actions into one report. A design estimate—not a measurement—is saving five minutes on each of ten weekly screenings, or about fifty minutes a week. Live collection and human verification are excluded from that estimate. Unclear sponsorship produces a question for networking time; missing skill evidence suggests an artifact for credibility-building time. Nothing is sent or published automatically.

## Failure modes and limits

**Role leakage:** a senior-engineer history is mistaken for entry-level analyst or designer support. Applicants changing tracks may miss the difference. Separate historical fields and opening-specific policies prevent a company total from overriding a role restriction; skill matching remains a limited token check.

**False absence/identity:** blanks or subsidiary names can exclude suitable employers, while similar names can create false matches. Applicants unfamiliar with corporate structures may not catch this. Exact-name matching blocks unresolved cases, and missing approvals remain unknown. The original H-1B join is not independently validated.

Liveness and timeline are hard gates. A closed gate yields Skip; unknown evidence yields Blocked. The prototype verifies file contents and its own execution, not current vacancies, visa eligibility, or hiring probability. A person must judge adequacy before applying. The small sample prioritizes an explainable decision boundary over an unsupported probability score.
