# Skill gap across three role families

## Executive summary

Across 573 US job postings from companies that have sponsored H-1B visas, the profile already shows evidence for the most-requested skills in the **Software engineer** family (42% of its top skill mentions). Skills asked for across all compared families: Distributed systems, Observability / monitoring, Python.

These are counts of what postings mention, not what jobs require. Which gap to learn first is the student's call.

## What this cannot tell

- Whether a skill is required: postings list more than a job needs on day one.
- Whether a skill is asked at the student's level: the shares count senior and non-senior postings together.
- Anything about a skill missing from the taxonomy, or companies whose boards are not on Greenhouse or Lever.
- Whether a posting is still open after the fetch date.
- Which gap matters most to learn next. That is the student's decision.
